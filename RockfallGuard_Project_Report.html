RockfallGuard Project Report & Guide

System: Proactive Open-Pit Mine Geological Risk Warning Platform

💡 What is RockfallGuard?

RockfallGuard is an Artificial Intelligence (AI) safety application for open-pit mining operations. It monitors ground sensors and live weather telemetry to predict dangerous rock wall collapses BEFORE they occur, giving mine workers enough time to evacuate safely.

1. The Problem We Solved

In open-pit mining, giant open rock faces are prone to sudden slope failures and rockfalls caused by rain saturation or ground pressure. Traditional alarm systems only sound after the ground is already collapsing. RockfallGuard uses Machine Learning (ML) to provide early warnings. The risk model is evaluated on synthetic data with temporal splits; see MODEL_CARD.md for metrics and limitations.

2. How RockfallGuard Works (Step-by-Step)

  • Step 1: Ground Sensors — Reads ground movement velocity (mm/h), acceleration (mm/h²), and underground water pressure (kPa).
  • Step 2: GPS Weather Integration — Uses the mine's exact GPS location (latitude & longitude) to fetch live rainfall from Open-Meteo.
  • Step 3: Machinery Noise Filter — Uses a 4th-order Butterworth digital filter to ignore heavy haul truck vibration noise, preventing false alarms.
  • Step 4: AI Failure Risk Score (0 - 100%):

    🟢 SAFE (<35%) Normal safe mining operations.

    🟡 WARNING (35-65%) Caution required; inspect slope bench.

    🔴 CRITICAL (>65%) DANGER! Evacuate lower bench sectors immediately!
  • Step 5: Automated Email & SMS Emergency Dispatches — When risk exceeds 80%, the system sounds sirens and automatically sends emergency emails and SMS text messages to mine safety officers.

3. Main Features Included

Feature Description
📊 Live Risk Score Gauge Displays real-time failure probability graphs and gauge updates every second.
🔍 SHAP Explainable AI Explains physical root causes behind risk spikes (e.g. "+2.31 Acoustic Acceleration").
🛸 Drone Photo Scanning PyTorch Deep Learning CNN inspects aerial drone photos for rock cracks.
🗺️ Interactive GPS Map Live OpenStreetMap satellite pin centered on the mine's geographic location.
➕ Register & Delete Mines Allows safety managers to add new pit mines or delete old ones from SQLite.
📱 Emergency Email & SMS Dispatches automated alerts to registered phone numbers and email addresses.

4. How to Use the Web Application

  1. Open http://127.0.0.1:8009 in your browser.
  2. Select your active mine from the dropdown menu.
  3. Use the scenario buttons (Normal, Heavy Rain, Machinery Noise, Emergency Alert) to test the system.
  4. Click 🛸 Drone CNN Inspection to test drone photo crack analysis.
  5. Click 🗑️ Delete to delete any mine from the system.
